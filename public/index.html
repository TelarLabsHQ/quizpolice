What QuizPolice does

Works with the quizzes you already have

Paste a Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or Wayground link. No need to rebuild your questions.

See who leaves the quiz

Switching tabs, opening another app or clicking outside the quiz is recorded with the time and how long they were away.

Lock after too many exits

Choose how many exits are allowed. Locked students wait for you to unlock them from your dashboard.

Live dashboard

A class list that updates every few seconds: answering, left the quiz, locked, or submitted.

Join with a code or QR

Students type a 6-digit code or scan the QR code. No student accounts needed.

Results you can keep

Download a CSV of names, sections, exits and time away for your records.

How it works in class

  1. Create the quiz

    Open the teacher dashboard, name your quiz and paste its link. Choose how many exits are allowed.

  2. Students join

    Show the code or QR on the board. Students type their name and section, then answer inside QuizPolice.

  3. Watch and act

    See exits as they happen, unlock students, and download the results when the quiz is done.

Honest about what it can and can't see

QuizPolice only knows when a student leaves the quiz page on the same device. It can't see other phones, the student's screen, or what they search. It's a strong reminder and a record, not mind-reading, and we tell students exactly what is monitored before they start.
