Privacy policy

QuizPolice is run by Telar Labs. We collect as little as possible and delete it quickly.

What we collect from students

  • The name and section the student types when joining.
  • Quiz activity: when they joined, left the quiz page, came back, were locked or unlocked, and tapped "I've submitted".

We do not collect quiz answers (those go directly to the teacher's form), email addresses, location, or anything that happens outside the quiz page.

What we collect from teachers

The quiz name, quiz link and settings. There are no teacher accounts; a secret key stored in the teacher's browser gives access to the dashboard.

Who can see it

Only the teacher who created the quiz (the browser holding its secret key). We don't sell or share the data.

How long we keep it

Everything about a quiz is deleted automatically 30 days after it was created. Teachers can delete a quiz and its results earlier from the dashboard.

Advertising

The information pages of this site may show ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies; you can manage personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. The student quiz screen and the teacher dashboard never show ads.

Children

QuizPolice is used in schools, including by students under 18. We only process the minimum needed for the teacher to run the quiz, and teachers are responsible for following their school's rules about using online tools with their students.

Hosting

The site and data are hosted on Cloudflare.
