How to use it

  1. Choose a ready setup, or open Grading setup to make your own: name your categories (homework, quizzes, labs, exams…) and give each a weight. A category can be split into parts, like Midterm 40% and Final 60%.
  2. Pick a grade scale: letters A to F, letters with GPA points, numbers only, or your own labels with the lowest grade for each. Add a pass mark if your school has one.
  3. Type the scores as “score out of total”, like 18 out of 20. Add as many as you need. The calculator adds them up, so a 50-point test counts more than a 10-point quiz in the same category.
  4. Read the grade. If some work isn’t graded yet, you’ll see the grade so far and what you need on the rest to reach your target.
  5. More than one term? Set the number of terms to get the final grade, with equal or different weights.

Share the same setup with your class or department

Tap Copy setup link and send it. Everyone who opens it gets your categories, weights, scale and terms, ready to type their own scores. Students can check their grade the same way you compute it. The link carries the setup only. Scores stay on each person’s own device, and nothing is stored on our servers.

How the grade is computed

  1. Category score = total points earned ÷ total points possible × 100. For a category with parts, each part is scored on its own, then combined by its weight.
  2. Weighted score = category score × its weight. The grade is the sum of the weighted scores.
  3. Conversion (optional). Some schools convert this “initial grade” with a table, also called transmutation. You can use a ready table or type your own.
  4. Final grade = the average of the term grades, or their weighted average if the terms count differently.

Examples

Weighted course: homework 92% (20%), quizzes 85% (30%), projects 88% (20%) and a final exam 80% (30%): 18.4 + 25.5 + 17.6 + 24.0 = 85.5%, a B on the A to F scale.

Three terms with a conversion table: with the K to 12 three-term setup for Grades 4–10 Math, Written Works 82%, Performance Tasks 80% and Exams 85.6% give 16.40 + 40.00 + 25.68 = an initial grade of 82.08, which the 2026–2027 adjusted table converts to 85 (Benchmarking). Questions

Is this an official calculator?

No. QuizPolice is an independent tool and is not affiliated with any school system or government agency. The ready setups follow published rules as closely as we can, but small differences can come from how a school rounds scores. Always check against your school’s official records.

Can I change a ready setup?

Yes. Pick it, then open Grading setup and change anything: names, weights, labels, the pass mark or the conversion table.

Are my scores saved?

Only in your own browser, so they’re still here when you come back on the same device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Can I print it?

Yes. Tap Print for a clean page with the scores and the grade.
