Test set shuffler

How to type the questions
  • Leave a blank line between questions.
  • Put each choice on its own line, starting with a letter: a), A. or (a).
  • Mark the correct choice with a * at the end, or add a line Answer: B. For questions without choices, Answer: photosynthesis goes into the key.
  • Start a line with # for a part heading, like # Part II. True or False. Questions are shuffled within each part, and parts keep their order.
  • Choices like “All of the above”, “Both A and B” or True/False keep their order.
  • Tip: copy your questions from Word or Google Docs and paste them here.

Why use different test sets?

When students sit close together, a different order of questions and choices makes copying from a neighbor much harder. Set A’s answer to number 5 is a different question on Set B. Combined with an exam seating chart that puts different sets side by side, it’s one of the simplest ways to keep a paper test fair.

How it works

  1. Paste your questions with their choices, and mark the answers with a *.
  2. Choose 2 to 6 sets. Each set gets its own order of questions, and of choices within each question.
  3. Print all the sets, one at a time, and the answer keys. Each key also shows the question’s original number, which helps with item analysis.

Tips

  • Keep Set A in the original order so it matches your table of specifications.
  • Write choices so they make sense in any order. Avoid “the answer above” or “see number 3”.
  • For matching-type or essay parts, put them in their own part (with #) and keep it short, or add them by hand after printing.
  • Giving the test online? Google Forms and Microsoft Forms can shuffle questions themselves, and QuizPolice shows you when students leave the quiz.

Questions

Are my questions uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Your questions are saved only on this device so they’re here next time.

Can I make the same sets again?

Yes. The sets stay the same until you tap Shuffle again, even if you close the page.
