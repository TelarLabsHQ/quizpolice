What changed

Under the old table (2015), an initial grade of 60 was transmuted to a passing 75. Under the adjusted table for SY 2026–2027, an initial grade of 70 is needed for 75. From SY 2027–2028, Grades 4 to 12 drop transmutation entirely: an initial grade of 75 is a final 75. Kindergarten to Grade 3 move to descriptive ratings over the same period.

The stated aim of the change is to make grades reflect what learners actually know, not to make passing harder. Teachers can plan support earlier when grades are more accurate.

How to use the table

  1. Compute the initial grade: the sum of the weighted scores of Written Work, Performance Tasks and Exams.
  2. Find the band it falls in, and read the transmuted grade.

Easier: the K to 12 grade calculator does all the steps for you, from raw scores to the term and final grade.

Always confirm with your school’s official e-Class Record.
