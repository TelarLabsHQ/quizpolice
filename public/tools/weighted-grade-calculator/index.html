How a weighted grade works

Most courses don’t count everything equally. A final exam might be worth 30% of the grade while homework is worth 20%. A weighted grade multiplies each category’s score by its weight, adds them up, and divides by the total weight.

Example: homework 92% (20%), quizzes 85% (30%), projects 88% (20%) and a final exam 80% (30%): 18.4 + 25.5 + 17.6 + 24.0 = 85.5%, a B on a common scale.

What score do I need on my final?

Required final score = (the grade you want − current grade × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight. For example, to finish with 90% when you have 86% and the final counts for 30%, you need (90 − 86 × 0.7) ÷ 0.3 = 99.3%.

Tips

  • Check your syllabus for the exact weights; categories don’t always add up to 100%.
  • Leave a score empty for work that isn’t graded yet. The calculator shows your grade so far from what’s done.
