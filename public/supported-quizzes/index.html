Supported quizzes

QuizPolice doesn’t replace your quiz. It shows the quiz you already made inside a monitored page. So the quiz has to allow being shown inside another site, and it must not ask students to sign in.

PlatformWorks?Settings to check
Google FormsYesTurn off Restrict to users in your organization and Collect email addresses: Verified. Use Responder input for emails instead. Signing in inside another site often fails because browsers block the needed cookies.
Microsoft FormsYesSet Who can fill out this form to Anyone can respond. "Only people in my organization" requires a sign-in that may not work inside QuizPolice.
WaygroundDependsSome Wayground pages don’t allow being shown inside other sites. Use Check this link when you create the quiz; if it’s blocked, use a Google or Microsoft form instead.
Other sitesMaybeAny quiz page that allows embedding will work. Check this link will tell you.

Why sign-in is the usual problem

When a quiz asks students to sign in, the sign-in page needs cookies from Google or Microsoft. Most browsers block those cookies when the page is shown inside another site, which is exactly how QuizPolice shows your quiz. The student sees a sign-in loop or a blank box instead of the questions. Turning off the sign-in requirement fixes it, and students type their name in QuizPolice instead, so you still know who answered.

Google Forms, step by step

  1. Open the form and go to Settings.
  2. Under Responses, set Collect email addresses to Do not collect or Responder input, not Verified.
  3. If you use a school Google account, turn off Restrict to users in [your school] and its trusted organizations.
  4. Leave Limit to 1 response off, because it also needs a sign-in. To stop repeat submissions, check names on your QuizPolice dashboard and in the form’s responses instead.
  5. Copy the link with Send → link icon, and paste it in QuizPolice.

Our guide to Google Forms quiz settings that reduce cheating explains the other settings worth turning on, like shuffling questions and releasing grades later.

Microsoft Forms, step by step

  1. Open the form and choose Settings (the three dots, then Settings).
  2. Under Who can fill out this form, choose Anyone can respond.
  3. Copy the link from Collect responses, and paste it in QuizPolice.

Shuffling questions, a start and end date, and showing results later all keep working; they are settings of the form itself.

Wayground and other sites

Whether a page can be shown inside another site is decided by that site, not by QuizPolice. Some quiz platforms allow it for some pages and block it for others, and the rules can change. That’s why QuizPolice has Check this link: it tries the link and tells you if it will load for students. If it’s blocked, copying the questions into a Google or Microsoft form is usually the quickest fix.

Before quiz day: a 2-minute check

  • Press Check this link when you create the quiz.
  • Open the join page on your own phone in a private window (not signed in), join with a test name, and answer one question.
  • Check that the test response appears in the form’s own Responses tab, then delete it.
  • On quiz day, show the 6-digit code or QR on the board and keep your dashboard open.

What students need

Any phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge, Samsung Internet or Firefox. No app to install and no accounts. Exits are detected most reliably in Chrome, Safari and Edge. Ask students to close other apps first and to keep their phone charged, because a phone that switches off counts as leaving the quiz.
