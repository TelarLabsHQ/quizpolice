For students

Before your quiz

Got a few minutes before your quiz starts? Read this quick checklist so nothing surprises you, then warm up with a practice game.

The 2-minute checklist

  • Battery: at least half charged, or plug in your charger now.
  • Internet: sit where your Wi-Fi or mobile signal is strong. Don’t start a big download.
  • Other apps: close games, videos and chat apps. Turn on Do Not Disturb so messages and calls don’t pop up.
  • Your name: type your full name exactly the way your teacher knows you, and choose the right section.
  • Paper and pencil: if your teacher allows scratch paper, have it ready so you don’t need another app.
  • Ask now: if you’re allowed to use a calculator or notes, check with your teacher before you start.

What QuizPolice can see, and what it can’t

Many teachers use QuizPolice to keep online quizzes fair. It’s good to know exactly what it does:

  • It can see when you leave the quiz page on the device you joined with: switching to another app or tab, going to your home screen, or clicking outside the quiz window. Your teacher sees when it happened and how long you were away.
  • It can’t see your screen, your photos, your messages, other devices, or what you search. Nothing outside the quiz page is monitored.

Your teacher may set a number of exits allowed. If you leave too many times, the quiz locks and you’ll need to ask your teacher to unlock it.

Things that can count as leaving, even by accident

  • Tapping a notification that pops up at the top of the screen.
  • Answering a call, or opening the calculator or camera app.
  • Swiping up to the home screen or switching apps with a gesture.
  • On a computer, clicking on another window or the taskbar.

That’s why Do Not Disturb matters. If something happens by accident, don’t panic: just come straight back and tell your teacher what happened.

During the quiz

  • Watch the timer if your quiz has one. You’ll see reminders when time is almost up.
  • If the quiz doesn’t load, stay on the page and tell your teacher. Don’t open the quiz in another tab, because that counts as leaving.
  • If your internet drops, reconnect and open the quiz page again. If it asks, join with the same name. Then tell your teacher.

When you finish

  1. Press Submit on the quiz itself (in Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or Wayground) and wait for the confirmation message.
  2. Then press I’ve submitted in QuizPolice, so your teacher sees that you’re done.

For a writing task, press Hand in my writing when you’re finished.

Early? Warm up

A short warm-up gets your brain going. Try a 60-second Math Sprint or a round of Geography Practice. Just make sure you’re back on the quiz page before it starts.

Good luck!

Take a slow breath, read every question to the end, and do your best. You’ve got this.
