About QuizPolice

QuizPolice is a free tool for teachers who give quizzes online, made by Telar Labs, a small independent studio.

Many teachers already use free Google Forms or Microsoft Forms for quizzes, but those can't tell when a student switches to another tab to search for answers. Exam lockdown tools are usually expensive, need installs, or require accounts for every student. QuizPolice adds a simple, honest layer of monitoring to the quizzes teachers already have.

Our promises

  • Free for teachers and students, no accounts.
  • We collect only what the teacher needs: name, section and quiz activity.
  • Student data is deleted after 30 days.
  • No ads on the student quiz screen or the teacher dashboard.
