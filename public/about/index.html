About QuizPolice

QuizPolice is a free tool for teachers who give quizzes online, made by Telar Labs, a small independent studio.

Many teachers already use free Google Forms or Microsoft Forms for quizzes, but those can’t tell when a student switches to another tab to search for answers. Exam lockdown tools are usually expensive, need installs, or require accounts for every student. QuizPolice adds a simple, honest layer of monitoring to the quizzes teachers already have.

Who it’s for

  • Classroom teachers who give short quizzes on phones or laptops and want to know who stays on the quiz.
  • Schools with mixed devices, where some students use phones, some use tablets and some use the computer lab. It runs in any modern browser.
  • Teachers giving homework quizzes in an open period, who want a record of time spent away from the quiz.
  • Tutors and trainers who run short assessments in seminars or review classes.

What makes it different

  • It works with the quiz you already have. You don’t rebuild questions in a new system. Answers keep going to your own form, so grading works the same way.
  • No student accounts. Students type a 6-digit code and their name. A class can start in under a minute.
  • Honest by design. Students are told what is recorded before they start. It records leaving the quiz page, not their screen, camera or microphone.
  • Records, not verdicts. An exit is a reason to ask, not proof of cheating. The dashboard shows the time and how long each student was away, so you can talk to the student with facts.

How we decide what to build

We add features that solve everyday classroom problems: timers and scheduled exams, giving one student extra time, unlocking a student during the quiz, and in-class essays with DraftTrail. We keep the teacher’s screen simple enough to use while walking around a classroom on a phone, and we design for low-cost Android phones and slow mobile data, because that’s what many students have.

Our promises

  • Free for teachers and students, no accounts.
  • We collect only what the teacher needs: name, section and quiz activity.
  • Student data is deleted after 30 days.
  • No ads on the student quiz screen or the teacher dashboard.
