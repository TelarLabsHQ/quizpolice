How QuizPolice works

Your quiz stays on Google Forms, Microsoft Forms or Wayground. QuizPolice shows it inside a page that notices when a student leaves.

What counts as leaving

  • Switching to another browser tab or window.
  • Opening another app on a phone or tablet, or going to the home screen.
  • Clicking outside the quiz on a computer (for example, into another window side by side).

Each exit is recorded with the time, and when the student comes back, how many seconds they were away.

Locking

With Lock after turned on, a student who reaches the exit limit sees "Quiz locked" and can't continue until you press Unlock on your dashboard. You can change the limit during the quiz.

Timers and schedules

When you set up a quiz, choose When do students take it?

  • Anytime: students start whenever you share the code. You can add a time limit (10 minutes to 3 hours) that starts when each student joins.
  • Scheduled exam: pick the date, start time and length. Students who open the link early wait on a countdown screen. The quiz appears for everyone at the start time and ends for everyone together. Students who join late get only the time that is left.
  • Open period: pick an opening and a closing date, for example Monday 8 am to Friday 5 pm. Students can start any time in between, with an optional time limit for each student that never runs past the closing time.

Students see a countdown above the quiz, with reminders at 5 minutes and 1 minute left. When time runs out, the quiz is covered with a Time's up screen. On your dashboard you see each student's time left, and you can Start now, give one student +5 minutes, or End the quiz for everyone. Times are set in your time zone; students see them in their own.

Important: QuizPolice can't press Submit on the Google or Microsoft form for the student. So by default, when time runs out students get 1 more minute to press Submit, with a red "Submit now" warning, and anything submitted then is marked late on your dashboard. You can choose 2 minutes, or close the quiz immediately. If a student is covered by "Time's up" before submitting, their answers stay on their screen as long as they don't refresh: give them +5 minutes and the quiz comes back with the answers still filled in. For a hard cut-off, you can also turn off "Accepting responses" on the form itself.

What it can't see

  • Another phone or a book on the desk.
  • Screenshots, or what is happening on the student's screen.
  • Whether the student actually pressed Submit on the form. Students tap I've submitted; check the form's own responses for the official answers.

Tips for fewer problems

  • Use Check this link when you set up the quiz. It warns you if the quiz requires sign-in or can't be shown inside other sites.
  • Ask students to close other apps before joining and to keep the phone plugged in.
  • Tell students that leaving is recorded. Most cheating stops once students know.
