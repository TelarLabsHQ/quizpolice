    How to play

    1. Choose a level. Start with an easy one to warm up.
    2. Press Start. You have 60 seconds.
    3. Type the answer with the number pad, or with your keyboard on a computer. A correct answer moves on by itself.
    4. Not sure? Type your best guess and press ✓ to move on. You’ll see the right answers at the end.

    The levels

    • Add to 20 and Subtract to 20: number facts that young learners use every day.
    • Add & subtract to 100: two-digit sums and differences, good practice for mental math.
    • Times tables: every table from 2 to 12, mixed together.
    • Division: the times tables backwards, always with whole-number answers.
    • Mix it up: a bit of everything, so you have to think about which operation to use.

    Why a 60-second sprint helps

    When basic facts like 7 × 8 come to mind instantly, your brain has more room for the hard part of a problem: understanding the question, planning the steps and checking the answer. Short, frequent practice is the best way to get there. A few sprints a day for a week does more than one long session.

    The sprint also shows you exactly what to practice. The list at the end only includes facts you got wrong, so you can write them on a card and try them again tomorrow.

    Tips to beat your best score

    • Accuracy first, then speed. A wrong answer costs more time than a careful right one.
    • Learn the tricky facts on purpose. For most people these are 6 × 7, 6 × 8, 7 × 8, 8 × 12 and the 9s.
    • Use patterns. 9 × 7 is 10 × 7 minus 7. 12 × 6 is 10 × 6 plus 2 × 6.
    • Play the same level a few days in a row. Your best score is saved on this device, so you can see yourself improve.

    For teachers and parents

    Math Sprint is free, needs no sign-up and works on phones, tablets and computers. Use it as a warm-up at the start of a lesson, as a quick homework task (“three sprints a day, write down your best score”), or as a calm activity at home. Scores stay on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

    Questions

    Is it free? Yes, completely.

    Does it save my score? Your best score for each level is saved in this browser only. Clearing your browser data removes it.

    Why are there no negative numbers? The subtraction levels always give answers of zero or more, so they match what most students learn first.
